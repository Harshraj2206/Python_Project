Indian Agricultural Season Guide

When each of the 22 crops AgriSmart knows is grown

Kharif June – October

Sown with the monsoon (June–July), harvested Sept–Oct.

Crops: Rice, Maize, Cotton, Jute, Pigeon Peas.
Rabi October – April

Sown in winter (Oct–Nov), harvested March–April.

Crops: Chickpea, Kidney Beans, Lentil.
Zaid March – June

Short summer season between Rabi and Kharif (March–June).

Crops: Watermelon, Muskmelon, Mung Bean, Black Gram.
Perennial Year-round

A tree or plantation crop that grows for many years.

Crops: Banana, Mango, Grapes, Apple, Coconut, Coffee.
